Exam season at Midnight Oil

Studying alone is hard. Every week we host nights that make it easier: silent sessions, group reviews and help from older students.

Three hours of total silence in the whole cafe. Phones go into a box at the door, and you get a free black tea every hour you stay.

Third- and fourth-year students sit at the group tables and help with maths, programming and English. Bring your questions.

A night for programmers: work on your project, show it to others, and get feedback. The best project of the month gets free coffee for a week.

Book a group table for up to six and review for the week's exams together. A whiteboard and markers are on every table.
