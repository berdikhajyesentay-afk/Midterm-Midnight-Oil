About us

Why we stay open late

Midnight Oil started in 2024, when four students could not find a single quiet place in the city after 22:00. The library closed, the cafes were loud, and the dorm was full of noise.

So we built the place we needed: warm lamps instead of bright lights, a socket at every seat, a quiet zone where nobody talks, and coffee that is still hot at 2 a.m.

  1. 18:00 The lamps go on, the first students arrive.
  2. 21:00 The quiet zone is full. Phones on silent.
  3. 00:00 Midnight soup for everyone still here.
  4. 03:00 Last coffee. Good luck tomorrow!
